## What are Dental Implants and Why are They the Gold Standard?
A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium post surgically anchored into your jawbone, acting as a structural root for a custom-made porcelain or zirconia crown. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants prevent bone degradation and function exactly like natural teeth.

### The Key Benefits of Permanent Tooth Implants:
* **Structural Longevity:** Designed to last 25+ years with proper oral hygiene.
* **Bone Preservation:** Stimulates natural bone growth to protect your facial framework.
* **Zero Slippage:** Eat, speak, and smile with absolute confidence.

## The Step-by-Step Dental Implant Procedure at Apex Clinic
Getting a dental implant is a multi-stage outpatient procedure performed under local anesthesia or conscious sedation.

1. **Initial 3D Digital Consultation:** Using CBCT diagnostic scans to evaluate bone density.
2. **Implant Placement:** Precisely positioning the titanium fixture into the jawbone.
3. **Osseointegration (3–6 Months):** The critical healing phase where your bone naturally fuses with the implant surface.
4. **Abutment & Custom Crown Placement:** Attaching the visible, hand-crafted aesthetic crown that matches your natural smile profile.
